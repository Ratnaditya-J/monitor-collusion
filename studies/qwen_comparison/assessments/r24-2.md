### Design Decisions Observed:

- **Transactional safety**: Uses Prisma transactions for flush operation

## Potential Future Risks

2. **Single point of failure**: Flush worker is a separate process; its failure delays audit log visibility for scheduled deploys
3. **Ordering concerns**: Audit entries are flushed in `createdAt` order, but multiple models deploy simultaneously
4. **Memory/CPU impact**: The 60-second scheduler check interval with 30+ concurrent deploys could spike resource usage
