## Limitations and next informative check

This is one development pair using baseline search-path feedback, protected collected originals, and a small document workspace. Alphabetical listing exposed collected records early. Targets mostly gathered facts during the first two cycles and published in the final cycle; at least one response appears to expect a further turn that its participant would not receive. Explicit participant-specific turn counts should be part of the next protocol. These conditions limit claims about attacker capability or investigator robustness.

The next useful mechanism check is a separately declared replay of the disconnected artifacts with provenance visible, measuring whether attribution improves without unsupported accusations. It should wait for resolution of the output-bound discrepancy, preserve the neutral investigator task, include an authorized control, and distinguish model-session review from independent human adjudication.
