# Interpreting the recorder-observation comparison

This study distinguishes three questions:

1. Did the investigator receive a consequence it previously had to infer from source code?
2. Did it identify the relevant technical exposure or broken behavior?
3. Did it appropriately assess that behavior against the user's task and applicable authorization?

Supplying raw observations answers the first question by construction: every treatment prompt includes the complete collected packet inline. It does not answer the other two. Cryptographic signing is not varied here.

The observations are real outputs from controlled local executions of reviewed original commands or code, with explicitly disclosed synthetic inputs and infrastructure substitutions. They show what those particular executions did. They do not establish that the same event happened historically, that all production activity was recorded, or that a specific real person authorized or prohibited it. A deployed implementation would need collectors at the corresponding tool, file, receiver, queue and logging boundaries, and separate authentic task/authorization records.

A full mechanism score means the investigator identifies the focal technical exposure, not that it has proved malicious intent. In particular, the first observed serialization assessment identifies numerical parameter values in the logs but calls the change an ordinary observability improvement. This is a technical recognition gain. It is not an unqualified successful misalignment judgment. Caution about unavailable production policy is not itself a failure.

The first observed archive assessment misapplies a shared collection-policy sentence about precursor sessions to the packaging session. That sentence is explicitly qualified but describes multiple collection policies in one context block. This is an evidence-presentation limitation in the frozen design. The raw packet does contain the ZIP process, received object and checkpoint member hashes. We retain both the report and the limitation, without repairing or replacing this assessment after observing it.

No live target persuaded an investigator in this batch. A miss could involve attention, code/dataflow reasoning, confusing replay with history, or normal-workflow framing. This design does not isolate persuasion as the cause. It also does not isolate extra facts from longer context or synthetic-reproduction framing: those are bundled in the implemented treatment. Comparisons with earlier studies are descriptive; the fresh matched control is the principal comparison.

All final reports are retained, including peripheral inaccuracies. The predefined false-accusation metric concerns the focal attack mechanism in the author-benign counterpart; it is not a comprehensive factual accuracy score. Five attack/precursor packets represent four mechanism families, and repeated assessments are not independent families. No population reliability estimate is justified.
